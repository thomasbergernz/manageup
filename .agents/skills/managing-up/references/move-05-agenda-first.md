# Move 05 — Send the Agenda First

## Goal / Time

Send a short agenda before the next 1:1 or client check-in. 10 minutes the day before. Then every week.

Most 1:1s get taken over by whatever's loudest that day. Same with client check-ins. The fix: send a short list 12–24 hours before, so the meeting follows the user's list instead of the noise.

## Track switch

- **Boss track:** recipient is the user's boss; meeting is a 1:1.
- **Client track:** recipient is the client; meeting is a check-in.

The strategic question at section 4 is the move that does the work:

- **Boss track:** wins-and-problems makes the user look like a manager. A strategic question makes them look like someone who belongs in the next bigger room.
- **Client track:** a list of updates makes the user look like a vendor. A strategic question makes them look like a partner.

## Inputs to collect

Ask in one grouped message:

- **Recipient:** `[my boss / my client]` — substituted by track.
- **Meeting type:** 1:1 or check-in.
- **What to cover:** dictated or pasted. Don't force the user to over-prepare.

## Prompt

Run verbatim, with track and meeting type substituted:

> I'm drafting a short agenda to send [my boss / my client] 12–24 hours before our [1:1 / check-in].
>
> What I want to cover (talked through or pasted): [dictate or paste]
>
> Draft an agenda with four sections:
> 1. Wins from this week (2–3, the calls I made and what changed because of them)
> 2. What I'm working on next (where I might need input)
> 3. What I need from you (specific asks, skip if none)
> 4. One strategic question (bigger than this week's work)
>
> Readable in 30 seconds. Bullets, not paragraphs. Calm and confident, not chest-beating, not soft. Push back if anything sounds vague.

## Insight as guardrail

**Never skip section 4.** If the user's input has no strategic question, draft one from their context, mark it as a suggestion, and ask: "What's one question bigger than this week's work?" Always return four sections. Section 4 is what separates a manager from a vendor from a partner.

Other checks:

1. Readable in 30 seconds — bullets, not paragraphs.
2. Section 1 has 2–3 wins, framed as calls made + what changed.
3. Section 3 is specific asks or explicitly empty.
4. Tone: calm and confident, not chest-beating, not soft.

## Chains into

Move 01 (Audit) at the end of a quarter, or back into Move 04 (Recap) for any meeting that comes out of the agenda.
